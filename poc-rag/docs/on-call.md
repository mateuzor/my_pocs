# On-call and incidents

The engineering team has a weekly on-call rotation. The rotation changes every Monday at 10:00 Lisbon time.

When production is down, page the on-call engineer using the PagerDuty app. Do not send a message in Slack first.

On-call engineers receive 150 euros per week of on-call, plus one extra day off after a week with more than 3 night incidents.

After every severity 1 incident, the team writes a blameless post-mortem within 5 business days.
