# Remote work policy

Acme Labs is remote-first. You can work from home every day, and the office in Lisbon is optional.

You can work from another country for up to 30 days per year. You must tell HR 10 days before you travel, because of tax and legal rules.

The company pays a home office stipend of 600 euros, one time, when you join. You can buy a chair, a desk, or a monitor.
