# Learning and growth

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per year. It covers courses, books, and conferences.

You also get 5 paid learning days per year to attend events or study. Your manager does not need to approve these days, but you should add them to the team calendar.

Certifications are paid in full if they are related to your role. The company asks you to stay for 12 months after a certification above 1,000 euros.
